# Code of Conduct

CivicBaseline follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) in spirit:

- Be respectful in issues, reviews, and operator forums.
- No harassment, and no pressure on utility or hospital staff to share live process data in public.
- Do not romanticize attacks on critical infrastructure.

Maintainers may reject contributions or ban participants who violate [ACCEPTABLE_USE.md](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md) or this code.

For conduct reports, use the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
