# CivicBaseline 0.1.0 (2026-09-18)

Initial public alpha:

- Evidence CLI (`civicbaseline assess`) with Markdown, HTML, JSON, and SARIF output
- CPG 2.0 **theme** pack (original wording) covering GOVERN through RECOVER
- Sector overlays: EPA water checklist themes, healthcare SSG themes, energy distribution/DER SSG themes
- CycloneDX and SPDX ingest; CISA KEV identifier matching (bundled sample or `--fetch-kev`)
- Optional passive PCAP parser (cleartext admin ports and labeled cross-zone flows)
- Synthetic fixtures: water plant, rural hospital, distribution substation
- CI workflow and release-artifact stub (project SBOM + impact snapshot)

This is a gap assistant, not a regulatory certification.
